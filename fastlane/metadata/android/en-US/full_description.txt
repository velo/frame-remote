A remote for Samsung Frame TVs (Tizen) that talks to the TV directly over your local network. No Samsung account, no SmartThings, no cloud round-trip, no tracking. Commands reach the TV in about 20 ms.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Exact volume slider (0–100, matches the TV's on-screen number) with −/+ steps and mute</li>
<li>Art Mode toggle that shows the TV's current art state</li>
<li>Wake-on-LAN for a TV in standby</li>
<li>D-pad, OK, Back and Home</li>
<li>One-tap Plex and YouTube launch, highlighted when that app is on screen</li>
<li>Finds the TV automatically on the network (SSDP), with manual IP entry as a fallback</li>
</ul>

On first use, the TV asks once to allow the remote. The pairing token stays on the phone.

Tested on a 2024 65" The Frame (QN65LS03D). It should work on most recent Samsung Tizen TVs.
